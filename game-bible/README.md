# The Siren — Game Bible v0.2

**Working project name:** The Siren (not final)  
**Updated:** 2026-10-23  
**Status:** Preproduction / solo React Three Fiber prototype

A narrative sci-fi mystery RPG about a stranded crew, a song that guides ships into ancient transportation spheres, and a cosmic conflict whose participants may not be what they seem.

## How to use this repository

- `vision/` defines the intended experience, not immutable lore.
- `narrative/` separates what is true, what the player learns, and what remains uncertain.
- `design/` records proposed gameplay systems.
- `prototype/` is the practical source of truth for the *next thing to build*.
- `ideas/` holds uncommitted possibilities and discarded directions.
- `decision-log.md` tracks commitments and rationale.

## Status labels

- **Established direction:** User has expressed clear preference, though details can change.
- **Tentative:** Promising idea under exploration.
- **Unresolved:** Important unanswered question.
- **Superseded:** Older framing displaced by a newer preference.

**Important:** Brainstorming is not canon. In particular, whether either ancient being is benevolent, why the prison exists, and what the final song does are *not settled*.
