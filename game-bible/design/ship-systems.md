# Ship Systems

## Narrative role

The ship is the crew's home, initially damaged by a decaying transit system, then reconstructed by ancient machinery. It retains familiar spaces and scars but gains unfamiliar compartments, altered geometry, and a transit interface.

## Transit authorization — Strong direction

- Conventional piloting remains available to other crew members.
- Network transit requires the protagonist aboard; authorization is passive in normal use.
- Available destinations are constrained by discovered/unlocked network routes.
- The device may be transmitting information back into the network.
- Unknown: whether the device recognizes the human protagonist or the ancient memories they carry.

## Ship theft quest — Tentative

Thieves seize the ship at a populated alien port, but cannot use its network transit without the protagonist. They can still maneuver within the local system. The player must recover the vessel; thieves or their sponsors may try to capture the protagonist. Potential inversion: infiltrate the player's own familiar ship as hostile territory.

## Production scope

For prototype, implement a static ship environment and a scripted reconstruction payoff. No full piloting or procedural travel simulation required.
