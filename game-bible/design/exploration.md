# Exploration and Level Design

## Location structure

A few bespoke locations rather than a massive open galaxy. Each site has a memorable architectural identity, local culture or history, and a distinctive central puzzle. The player should learn the space through traversal and changing access routes.

## First facility

Wreckage → ancient corridors → surviving crewmate → resonance chamber → altered return route → reconstructed ship.

## Atmosphere

Contrast intimate, damaged ship interiors with incomprehensibly large ancient spaces. Use lighting, sound, scale, and environmental response to communicate wonder and dread.
