# Superseded or Deferred Ideas

These are not permanently banned; record why a direction was set aside.

- **Sphere intentionally shreds ships as a weapon — Superseded.** Preferred version: transportation machinery is decaying; destruction is unintended.
- **Protagonist literally is an ancient being with amnesia — Superseded.** Preferred version: a genuine modern human carrying inherited memories.
- **Imprisoned entity is definitely evil — Superseded.** New direction explores a potentially benevolent or morally complex being and an ancient war.
- **Escape the sphere then inexplicably end up far away — Superseded.** The sphere is a transport entrance; the remote facility is the destination.
- **Ship remains ruined indefinitely — Superseded.** First puzzle triggers mysterious reconstruction and transit upgrade.
- **Large open galaxy in first prototype — Deferred.** Focus on one excellent chamber and a contained narrative opening.
- **Full ship piloting, combat, advanced companion AI — Deferred.** Not required to validate the core experience.
