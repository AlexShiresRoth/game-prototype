# Characters — Working Roster

## Protagonist — Established direction

Human freelance crew member with an ordinary life and relationships; wants to get home. Carries inherited ancient memories, not a hidden ancient identity that negates their own personhood. **Tentative new lore:** related to a celebrated hero and lives under the pressure of that person's legacy, as an original thematic nod to Mass Effect. Parent, ancestor, missing relative, and controversial hero variants remain open.

## Original crew — Established direction, casting open

Small crew aboard a worn, lived-in vessel. Several are separated during the transit disaster; at least one survivor can be found during the opening. Roles to consider: pilot, engineer, scientist, communications/navigation specialist. Avoid finalizing exact number or deaths before the opening is paced.

## Surviving engineer — Tentative prototype role

Found beyond wreckage; assists with restoring access to the resonance chamber; reacts to the ship's impossible reconstruction. Could be another original crewmate instead.

## Apparent antagonist — Strong direction

A recurring individual who sabotages activations and appears to oppose the protagonist. Potentially a former operator whose successful activation caused disaster. They are trying to avert catastrophe, though their tactics may be dangerous. They may have their own agenda and be manipulated by the opposing ancient intelligence.

## Imprisoned intelligence — Strong direction, moral nature unresolved

Communicates indirectly through signals, machinery, and perhaps visions. Helps the protagonist while directing them toward locks. Not assumed evil. Could be fighting an ancient war, or be desperate for a solution to a real catastrophe.

## Opposing intelligence — Tentative

Another ancient force with an understandable but potentially harmful worldview. May influence the apparent antagonist; distinct from that mortal character.

## Other operators — Strong direction

Different civilizations supply compatible individuals. Archetypes: one who succeeded and regrets it; one who failed; one who refuses; one still trusting the signal. Each has their own motivations.

## Crew expansion — Strong direction

Alien companions join through story events, adding perspectives and warmth to the ship. Relationships should be personal, not just lore-delivery devices.
