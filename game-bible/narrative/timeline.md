# Timeline — Relative, Unfinalized

## Deep past

- Ancient civilization rises and builds extraordinary technologies.
- Two intelligences or ideological factions may collaborate, diverge, or wage war.
- Containment network is constructed or repurposed; one intelligence is imprisoned.
- Civilization disappears; maintenance systems continue operating and decay.

## Before game

- Artificial spheres begin appearing near inhabited systems.
- Ships vanish after encountering siren signals.
- Other operators interact with distant locks, with mixed outcomes.
- The protagonist grows up under the possible shadow of a famous relative.

## Opening chapter

1. Freelance investigation contract.
2. Siren signal influences ship navigation.
3. Decaying sphere transports ship to selected facility; ship breaks apart.
4. Protagonist wakes, explores wreckage, searches for survivors.
5. Resonance puzzle activates maintenance protocol.
6. Ship is reconstructed and fitted with transit interface.
7. Crew discovers available destinations and begins the journey home.
