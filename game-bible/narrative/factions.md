# Factions and Interests — Tentative

- **Freelance crew:** Survival, reunion, home, scientific curiosity; differing opinions about trusting the network.
- **Ancient containment builders:** Vanished civilization; their intentions and reliability are unknown.
- **The imprisoned intelligence's influence:** Operators recruited or manipulated across different species and systems; not necessarily a unified organization.
- **The opposing intelligence's influence:** Could include followers, other operators, or legacy systems.
- **Corporate/research interests:** Contemporary organizations seeking to acquire ancient technology, potentially hiring thieves or mercenaries.
- **Ship thieves:** Local smugglers, opportunists, or contractors who can fly the ship conventionally but cannot initiate network transit without the protagonist.
- **Independent communities:** Civilizations affected by Objects, disasters, or operators; some may see the apparent antagonist as a protector.

Keep the universe socially varied. Not every antagonist is part of the ancient conflict.
