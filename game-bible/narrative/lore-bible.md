# Lore Bible

## Contemporary setting — Established direction

Humans inhabit interstellar space alongside alien civilizations. Social inequality, corporate ambition, and ordinary personal needs coexist with advanced technology. No single simplistic evil galactic empire is required.

## Artificial spheres / Objects — Established direction

Large, mysterious artificial spheres appear near inhabited systems. They emit structured frequency sequences that lure nearby ships by causing navigation systems to interpret the signal as valid guidance. The Objects were not originally designed to destroy vessels. They are part of an ancient transit/maintenance infrastructure, now decaying. A transfer can badly damage a ship while successfully relocating it.

## The first destination — Established direction

The protagonist's ship is intentionally routed to a particular remote facility by the imprisoned intelligence because it needs a compatible operator there. The destruction of the ship is an unintended consequence of decaying transport machinery, not the original purpose of the transit system.

## Ancient network — Established direction, details tentative

Multiple artificial sites are connected by transit and frequency-based machinery. Artificial puzzle planets may be distributed locks in a containment network. Ancient spheres may also service, power, or transport operators between sites. Dark matter harvesting/manipulation is a speculative option, not confirmed lore.

## Resonance and music — Established direction

Sequences of frequencies can affect ancient mechanisms and appear as musical fragments to the player. The same phenomenon can be interpreted differently by machines and minds. The original and corrupted sequences may differ by subtle harmonic changes. The full sequence's function is unresolved.

## The imprisoned intelligence — Revised / unresolved

Originally envisioned as an entity manipulating operators into weakening its prison. Newer direction: it may be benevolent, partly benevolent, or morally complex rather than inherently evil. It may be a participant in an ancient war with another intelligence. Its guidance is useful but incomplete and may still be manipulative. Its real objective and the moral legitimacy of its imprisonment remain unresolved.

## The opposing intelligence — Tentative

A second ancient being or faction may oppose the imprisoned intelligence. Their conflict could involve freedom versus preservation, radically different responses to cosmic catastrophe, or a shared system later repurposed as a prison. Neither account is automatically reliable. Alternative: the ancient war has already ended but automated systems continue it.

## Living operators — Established direction, mechanism unresolved

Ancient locks require compatible living operators. Objects appear near inhabited systems to find or recruit them. Different species can supply operators; not all attempts succeed. A possible mechanism is compatibility with cognitive/biological patterns rather than species-specific genetic keys. Why living authorization is required is unresolved.

## Protagonist's inherited memory — Established direction

The protagonist is genuinely a present-day human, not simply an ancient alien with amnesia. They carry memories/information from an individual belonging to the vanished civilization, perhaps a network architect or engineer. How this happened is unresolved. Their present agency must remain central.

## Ship reconstruction and transit — Established direction

After the first resonance puzzle, the facility reassembles the damaged ship and incorporates an ancient transit interface. The ship retains its lived-in identity, physical scars, and strange new features. Transit is limited to available network destinations and passively requires the protagonist aboard. Other crew can operate the ship normally. Whether authorization recognizes the human or the inherited ancient identity remains open.

## The approaching catastrophe — Established direction, cause unresolved

A looming cosmic danger raises the stakes of returning home. It may arise from failing locks, opening them, keeping them closed, or the ancient conflict itself. No real-time timer has been committed.

## Historical chronology — Broad only

1. An ancient civilization develops extraordinary physical technologies.
2. It encounters or creates powerful intelligence(s) and a conflict unfolds.
3. A distributed network is built or repurposed as containment infrastructure.
4. The civilization disappears; the system decays across immense timescales.
5. Objects begin appearing near contemporary inhabited systems and drawing in operators.
6. The protagonist's crew is lured into a sphere and transported to a selected remote facility.

The ordering and causation of events 1–4 remain tentative.

## Cross-species resonance language — tentative

Some patterns recur across ancient machinery, modern ship systems, cultural traditions, and possibly communication between otherwise mutually unintelligible species. Shared mathematical structures do not imply shared emotional interpretation. One hypothesis: ancient systems render complex signals into forms different species can perceive; the protagonist experiences them as music. Whether the ancient beings communicate this way naturally, and whether it predates the ancient civilization, are unresolved. See `design/musical-language.md`.
