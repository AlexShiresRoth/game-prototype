# Prototype Narrative — The Siren

**Working length:** 15–25-minute full opening concept; first playable slice should be much shorter.

## 1. Approach (scripted)

A freelance crew approaches an artificial sphere after other ships vanish. They detect an oddly musical frequency. Navigation begins interpreting the signal as flight corrections. The pilot loses control. The sphere initiates an ancient transfer; readouts indicate declining field coherence. The transfer succeeds but tears the ship apart because the mechanism is deteriorating.

## 2. Wreckage

The protagonist wakes inside a ruined vessel, with crew missing. Explore compartments, use interaction prompts, inspect damaged communications and sensor logs. A hatch reacts to a frequency or the protagonist's presence. A brief unfamiliar memory flashes through their mind.

## 3. Ancient interior

The protagonist leaves the wreck into a huge artificial facility. A surviving engineer or other crewmate is found; the pair establish that machinery responds to signals. The player restores access to the central chamber.

## 4. Resonance chamber

Three resonators respond to adjustments; clues reveal a sequence related to the siren signal. The player activates the system. Ancient memories intrude. The facility's maintenance protocol awakens.

## 5. Reconstruction and departure

The engineer reports impossible activity around the ship. Scattered hull pieces and damaged compartments reassemble. An alien transit device integrates with the vessel. The ship is restored but altered. The navigation display shows unfamiliar destinations; home is absent. The system will authorize travel only with the protagonist aboard. A subtle altered note or mysterious message hints that the sequence served someone else's purpose. The crew selects a destination and leaves.

## What player knows at the end

- The sphere lured them through machine-readable music.
- The catastrophic ship damage was associated with unstable transit.
- The protagonist is mysteriously compatible with the machinery.
- The ship has been rebuilt with a transit capability.
- The crew is stranded far from home and has only a few routes forward.

## What remains hidden

The entity deliberately selected the destination to recruit a compatible operator. The frequency puzzle may have activated part of a lock. The imprisoned intelligence may be benevolent, harmful, or neither; its opponent's account is also unverified.

## Sample dialogue (illustrative, not final)

Engineer, as machinery reconstructs ship: “That's not our engine.”

Engineer, after inspecting new routes: “Home isn't on the chart.”

Mysterious signal, perhaps: “I knew you would hear me.”
