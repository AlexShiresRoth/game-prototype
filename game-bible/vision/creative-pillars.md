# Creative Pillars

1. **Wonder with unease.** Environments inspire curiosity before revealing their dangers.
2. **The crew is home.** Relationships, mundane rituals, humor, and disagreement balance cosmic stakes.
3. **Mysteries are playable.** Observation and experimentation—not exposition dumps—advance understanding.
4. **Sound is a language.** Frequencies affect navigation, machinery, puzzles, and memory; musical motifs accrue meaning.
5. **No easy moral binary.** Ancient beings and human/alien factions have understandable motives and observable consequences, not automatically equal moral standing.
6. **Earned revelations.** Early clues support later reversals; don't rely on characters refusing to explain for no reason.
7. **Handcrafted scope.** A few memorable locations beat a vast procedurally empty galaxy.

## Avoid

- A conventional chosen-one destiny that erases the protagonist's agency.
- Every problem being secretly caused by one cosmic conspiracy.
- Punishing every puzzle completion with an unavoidable bad outcome.
- Frequency puzzles requiring perfect pitch or music training.
- A mandatory real-time catastrophe countdown that discourages exploration.

## Music as an emotional and narrative language (2026-10-23 expansion)

**Strengthened pillar:** Music is not only atmosphere or a lock-and-key puzzle; patterns gradually become a vocabulary for recognizing other beings and understanding the universe. The soundtrack can carry clues and emotional meaning. The player learns to interpret rather than perform. See `design/musical-language.md`. The precise metaphysics remain tentative.
