# Inspirations and Originality Notes

- **Mass Effect:** emotional crew bonds, galaxy-scale choices, the weight of a famous hero's legacy. A possible protagonist relative is a celebrated hero; keep the person and historical events wholly original.
- **Ocarina of Time:** puzzles embedded in environments, mechanisms that reveal new routes.
- **Majora's Mask:** melancholy and an approaching catastrophe without sacrificing intimacy.
- **The Expanse:** institutions, ordinary economic pressures, believable technical investigation, enigmatic alien phenomena.
- **Siren folklore:** a beautiful call that lures travelers into danger, reframed as machine-interpretable signal rather than supernatural enchantment.

Use original character designs, names, lore, music, dialogue, and visual assets. These references guide feeling and structure only.
