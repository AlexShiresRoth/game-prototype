# Game Overview

## High concept

A freelance spacecraft crew investigates a mysterious sphere emitting a structured siren-like signal. The signal deceives the ship's navigation systems into approaching; an ancient, deteriorating transit mechanism transfers the ship to a distant containment facility and catastrophically damages it. The protagonist survives, explores the facility, and solves a resonance puzzle. The facility reconstructs the crew's ship, incorporating a mysterious transit device that lets them travel between ancient sites—but only when the protagonist is aboard to authorize it. Their attempt to return home entangles them in a galaxy-spanning conflict between ancient intelligences and the machinery of a long-forgotten prison.

## Player fantasy

Explore extraordinary alien places; decipher physical and musical mysteries; build relationships with a found-family crew; make meaningful decisions under uncertainty; gradually understand an ancient network; find a way home.

## Format and ambition

- Narrative-led third-person 3D exploration RPG with handcrafted, Zelda-like puzzle locations.
- Companion conversations and ship-as-home interludes.
- Musical/frequency sequences are a unifying mechanic and narrative language.
- Solo playable prototype first; any larger studio or full RPG is a long-term possibility, not current production scope.

## Touchstones

Mass Effect: crew relationships, space opera, personal legacy. Ocarina of Time: interconnected puzzle environments and earned progression. Majora's Mask: melancholy, strange inhabitants, looming catastrophe. The Expanse: grounded human motivations against cosmic unknowns. These are tonal/mechanical inspirations, not borrowed intellectual property.

## Core promise

A mysterious, beautiful universe that can be frightening but never emotionally empty. The mystery matters because the people aboard the ship matter.
